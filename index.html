Defensive Warfare

Leader Strategy

#1 Position

1. Defensive warfare is a game only for the leader.

Protect your territory, institutional knowledge, and key accounts.

2. The best defense is the courage to attack yourself.

Continuously disrupt your own workflows before management forces you to.

3. Always block your competitors' strong moves.

Anticipate rivals trying to encroach on your projects and preemptively act.

4. Keep something in reserve.

Never burn all political capital on a single project. Save energy for crises.

⚔️

Offensive Warfare

Challenger Strategy

#2 Position

1. Gain relative advantage at decisive points.

Focus intensely on one critical skill or project where you can decisively outperform.

2. Find the leader's weak spot and attack it.

Observe where top performers drop the ball and excel specifically in those gaps.

3. Attack on the narrowest front possible.

Focus your campaign on a single highly visible initiative rather than spreading thin.

4. Strength Is Weakness.

Do not fight the leader on their strongest turf; win on quantifiable output instead.

🚀

Flanking Warfare

No. 3 Strategy

#3 Position

1. Pursuit is the second act of victory.

When the leader stumbles, position your supplementary work to highlight superior methods.

2. Target territory that is not yet contested.

Volunteer for emerging tech or niche problems that others ignore.

3. Tactical surprise must be central.

Deliver unexpected, fully realized solutions quietly before others realize a shift.

🥷

Guerrilla Warfare

Underdog Strategy

Underdog

1. Retreat when enemy advances; attack when tired.

Avoid political confrontations. Pick battles wisely and offer subtle constructive critiques.

2. Find a segment small enough to defend.

Carve out an essential, highly specific niche or tool that nobody else wants to manage.

3. Whatever success you achieve, never behave like leader.

Maintain humility and agility. Do not isolate yourself with bureaucracy or arrogance.

4. Be ready to pack up and move at any moment.

Keep options open. Pivot strategically if a project becomes toxic or stagnant.

About this App

Promotion Warfare System V05

Promotion Warfare is a free guide that helps you grow at a company. You map the people around you, find the strategy that fits your position and turn it into small weekly moves.

What is inside: a company power map and pyramid drawing, the Strategic Square, 4 strategies with 15 simple explanations, an interactive Action Plan, a Wins Journal, a Glossary, 6 example stories and a Career Adviser chat with 3,000 questions and answers.

About the Adviser: it is a smart search through a built-in data bank. It remembers what you talked about and understands different ways of asking, but it is not a human and not a live AI. It can look up new topics on the web when you ask it to.

The idea: the four strategies (defensive, offensive, flanking, guerrilla) are inspired by classic business strategy thinking and adapted to personal careers. The war words are only a metaphor: win by being useful, honest and respectful.

Please note: this is educational guidance, not legal, medical, financial or HR advice. Stories are made-up examples.

Privacy Policy

Your data stays on your phone

1. Who we are

Promotion Warfare is a free educational app made by HiProgress (Israel). Our shop is at hiprogress.etsy.com. This policy explains what the app does with your information. Last updated: 29 October 2026.

2. What stays on your phone

Everything you type or choose stays on your own device, in the app's local storage: your optional name, your avatar or photo, the people on your power map, your Action Plan, your Wins Journal, your chat with the Adviser, answers you add to the data bank and your theme and text-size settings. We do not receive this information and we cannot see it.

3. What we collect

Nothing. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no access to your contacts, location, microphone or camera.

4. Your profile photo

If you choose to upload a picture, the app shrinks it to a small square and stores it only on your phone. It is never uploaded anywhere. You can remove it at any time in Profile and Settings.

5. The Career Adviser

The Adviser answers from the built-in data bank inside the app. It works offline and your questions are not sent anywhere. It is a program that finds answers in its data bank. It is not a person and it is not a live AI service.

6. Optional web search

Only if you tap "Search the web", the words of your question are sent over the internet to Wikipedia (Wikimedia Foundation) to fetch a short summary. Wikipedia's own privacy policy applies to that request. No other service is contacted and nothing is sent unless you tap that button. You can save a result to your data bank on your phone.

7. Public libraries we load

To display the app, it loads the Tailwind CSS and Font Awesome files from public content delivery networks (CDNs). Like any website file request, this lets those providers see technical details such as your IP address when the files are first downloaded. After that the app keeps a copy for offline use.

8. Links and sharing

Links to our Etsy shop, to web results and to other sites open in your browser, where their own policies apply. When you use Save / Share on a picture or file, you choose where it goes through your phone's share sheet.

9. Age

The app is meant for adults, 18 years and older, and is not directed to children.

10. Your control

You can edit or delete your data in Profile and Settings. "Delete all my data" removes everything the app stored. Clearing the app's storage in your phone settings or uninstalling the app also removes it.

11. Not professional advice

The app gives general educational guidance about careers. It is not legal, medical, financial or human-resources advice. Example stories are made-up characters written for learning.

12. Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will appear in the app with a new date. For questions, contact us through the HiProgress shop on Etsy: hiprogress.etsy.com.
