Privacy Policy

App: Create Your New Product (the "App"), by HiProgress. Effective date: October 20, 2026.

Summary

The App does not collect, store on our servers, sell or share your personal data. There are no accounts, no ads and no analytics.

Information you enter

The answers, checklist ticks, numbers and notes you type into the App are saved only on your own device, in the app's private storage, so you can continue later. We cannot see them. If you use "Start over" or delete a product, the data is deleted.

Pictures and documents you attach

You can take a picture or choose files (for example a sketch picture, an Excel list or a Word document). These files are copied into the app's private storage on your device and are never sent to us or to anyone else. The App reads a picture or file only when you tap a button to add it.

Save files

When you tap "Save the Product", the App creates a TXT file with your product data and attached files and puts it in your device's downloads. That file belongs to you. You decide where to keep it or whether to share it. We cannot see it.

Business tools

The Business tools (price scenarios, invoices, suppliers, customers, inventory, reminders and others) save their data only on your device. PDFs and calendar files are created on your phone and are shared only when you choose to share them. We cannot see them.

Profile, sandbox and documents

Your avatar, name, sandbox text, document blocks and settings (such as dark mode) are saved only on your device. We cannot see them.

Sharing

The Share buttons on the results page send a short summary only when you tap one. They open the app or website you choose (for example email, WhatsApp or Facebook), which has its own privacy policy. Nothing is shared automatically.

Permissions

The App does not ask for access to your contacts, location, microphone or messages. It uses the camera or your files only when you choose "Take a picture" or an upload button.

Third-party links

The HiProgress logo in the App opens our online shop (hiprogress.etsy.com) in your browser. That website is run by Etsy and has its own privacy policy. We have no control over how it handles your data.

Estimates are not advice

Prices and profit figures are simple estimates based on numbers you enter. They are not financial, legal or tax advice.

Children

The App is intended for adults and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

Changes

If we change this policy, we will update the date above.

Optional internet search in the Adviser

When the Adviser cannot answer from the notes stored in the App, it offers a button "Search the internet (Wikipedia)". Only if you tap it, the words of your question are sent to Wikipedia (Wikimedia Foundation) to fetch a short public article text. Nothing else is sent, and we do not receive or store your question. If you tap "Add to my data bank", the text is saved only on your phone. Wikipedia text is available under the CC BY-SA licence and the source is shown with each answer.
